## The Agents: Grok Build, Grok Bot, and the Grok App

An agent's harness is the program around the model that lets it act: it reads files, runs commands, and asks your permission where it matters. xAI offers three. Grok Build works directly in a folder on your computer; Grok Bot works on a computer of its own in the cloud; and the Grok app can build small apps, use your connected accounts and run scheduled jobs. [AI Agents](../agents.md) explains agents in general, and [Your First Agent Session](../first-session.md) walks through a first task.

## Commands, with Worked Examples

In Grok Build you type a command starting with a slash, such as `/plan`, in the same box as your requests. A few start with `grok` instead, such as `grok inspect`; those are typed in an ordinary terminal, outside Grok Build. These are the commands a faculty member, student or staff member might use, grouped by what they help with. Each example is a small, realistic situation, what you type, and what you then see, drawn from xAI's documentation; where xAI does not quote the screen, the figure describes it. Some commands appear only when a feature is turned on. Commands meant for software developers are listed at the end, without examples. xAI documents no slash commands for the Grok app, and Grok Bot's few typed symbols are under Shortcuts and Symbols.
