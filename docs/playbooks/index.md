# Step-by-Step Guides

Each guide walks one task end to end with artificial intelligence (AI) at the American University of Antigua College of Medicine (AUACOM): the prompt it uses, the steps, and what to check before you rely on the result.
{: .guide-index-intro }

<div class="grid cards guide-index" markdown>

-   ### For faculty and staff

    - [Preparing a Lecture](lecture-prep.md): from objectives to a session plan with active-learning checks
    - [Writing and Vetting Exam Questions](exam-items.md): AI-assisted drafting and structural vetting of exam items, with accuracy and exam decisions kept with you and your exam committee
    - [Your Syllabus AI Statement](syllabus-statement.md): setting course-level AI expectations, with adaptable templates
    - [Feedback on Student Writing](writing-feedback.md): rubric-grounded formative feedback on a whole stack of submissions, in your own voice
    - [AI-Generated Images in Teaching](ai-images.md): why image generators fail at anatomy, what to reach for instead, and what the evidence says about using a flawed image on purpose
    - [Reviewing the Literature](literature-reviews.md): for faculty and graduate students, from question to appraised evidence, with citations that check out
    - [Administrative Drafting: Memos, Minutes, and Reports](admin-drafting.md): memos, minutes, and reports drafted from facts you supply, with the model doing the arranging

-   ### For students

    - [Shelf and NBME Score Reports](score-reports.md): for students and their advisors, from an exam performance report to an honest, interleaved study plan your advisor can hold you to
    - [AI and the Residency Application](residency-application.md): what the Association of American Medical Colleges (AAMC) and the Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates (ECFMG) actually permit, where AI helps in the application year, and where it does not, with a section for letter writers

</div>

The [Prompt Library](../prompts/index.md) gives you the words, and the [AI Literacy Pathway](../pathway/index.md) gives you the foundations. To request the next guide, use the [feedback form](https://forms.office.com/r/5a8RCi2YKP).
{: .guide-index-intro }
